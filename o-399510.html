Major John Clarence "Bucky" Egan

They admired Egan, a recognizable figure at Thorpe Abbotts bomber base with his thin mustache, trademark white fleece-lined flying jacket, habit of speaking in idioms, and easy camaraderie. When he wasn’t scheduled to fly, Egan would hang out in bars with local laborers into the wee hours of the morning. His big personality and easy manner meant he became instantly liked and respected by new crews.
